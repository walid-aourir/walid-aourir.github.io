2. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We practice strict data minimization. Depending on whether you use CallerID as a Guest or as an Authenticated Account Holder, we process only the following specific categories of data:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile Credentials: When you create an optional account, we collect your display name/username, international phone number normalized in E.164 format (e.g., +14155550199), two-letter ISO country code, optional profile avatar (stored as a compressed data URL if uploaded), and your chosen security PIN/password (which is cryptographically salted and hashed via Firebase Authentication and never stored in plaintext).
  • Profile Discoverability Preferences: Your explicit boolean toggles governing whether your profile name is searchable (`isProfileDiscoverable`), whether your last-known location is publicly discoverable (`isLocationDiscoverable`), and whether your identity is shared when you search another registered user (`shareSearcherIdentity`).
  • Community Spam Reports & Block Lists: When you report a phone number as spam or block a number, we process the target E.164 phone number, spam category (e.g., Telemarketer, Scam/Fraud, Robocall, Phishing), optional suggested caller label, and optional comment.

2.2 Information Collected Only With Explicit Device Permission

  • Last-Known Location Telemetry (Opt-In Only): If and only if you grant iOS Location Services permission (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription) and enable Location Sharing or accept a peer location request, we collect your latitude, longitude, horizontal accuracy radius (in meters), reverse-geocoded city/locality and country name, and timestamp.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you grant iOS Notification permission, we store your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token and notification category toggles so we can deliver real-time alerts (such as when someone searches your phone number or sends a location request).

2.3 Technical, Device & Subscription Metadata

  • Device Model Identifier: When you register or sign in, we record your hardware model name (e.g., iPhone 16 Pro) so it can be displayed on your profile and authorized lookup cards.
  • Subscription Entitlement Records: When you purchase or restore a subscription (Locator_weekly, Locator_monthly, or Locator_yearly), Apple StoreKit 2 provides signed transaction metadata (Product ID, transaction ID, active/expired status, and expiration timestamp) which our Firebase Cloud Function verifies to synchronize your ad-free Premium status. We never receive or store your credit card, bank account, or Apple Pay details.

3. Strict Consent-Based GPS Location & Turn-by-Turn Routing Policy

No Covert Surveillance or Unauthorized Tracking: CallerID is strictly a consent-based communication and safety utility. It cannot and does not track any phone number without the device owner’s explicit registration and authorization.

Our location features operate under four technical and legal guardrails:

  1. Dual-Layer Opt-In for Public Discoverability: By default, public location discoverability is turned off unless you grant iOS Location Services permission and explicitly confirm our in-app public disclosure alert acknowledging that other authenticated users who search your phone number or name may view your last-known location.
  2. Permissioned Peer-to-Peer Location Requests: If your public location discoverability is disabled, another authenticated user may only send you a location request notification. Your coordinates are shared only if you tap Accept & Share.
  3. Turn-by-Turn Route Calculation (`MapKit`): When you view a located number’s coordinates and request a driving or walking path, CallerID uses your current device location and Apple’s MKDirections framework to render a route polyline, distance, and estimated travel time. Your temporary routing origin used to render the map path is processed in memory and via Apple MapKit and is not published to the target user unless your own location sharing is enabled.
  4. Instant Revocation & Throttling: When location sharing is active, updates are throttled (at most once every 5 minutes while the app is active) to preserve battery and minimize data collection. You can disable location sharing at any moment in the Locator tab or Settings > Location, which immediately blocks further location lookups of your number.

4. Apple CallKit & Address Book Protection

  • No Unauthorized Contact Harvesting: CallerID does not scrape, upload, or sell your private iPhone Contacts or Address Book.
  • On-Device Call Directory Extension: When you block a phone number or sync spam protection, CallerID writes E.164 numerical identifiers to a secure shared App Group container (group.com.calleddetector.callerid.shared) on your device and reloads Apple’s native CXCallDirectoryProvider. iOS matches incoming calls locally on your device against that list without exposing your live call log to our servers.
  • No Call Audio Interception: CallerID never listens to, records, or transcribes your phone conversations.

5. “Who Searched My Number” & “Who Blocked My Number” Disclosures

  • Search Activity Logging: When a user performs a phone number lookup or location lookup on a registered CallerID user, our backend increments the target user’s search activity counter and logs a search event (lookup type: number or location, timestamp, and—if the searcher is authenticated and has Share My Identity When Searching enabled—the searcher’s display name, phone number, and country). You may anonymize your outbound searches at any time in Settings > Search Privacy.
  • Honest Technical Disclosure for “Who Blocked My Number”: Apple iOS and telecommunications carriers do not expose private device-level block lists to any third-party application. Accordingly, our “Who Blocked My Number” feature exclusively displays voluntary community-submitted block reports logged within the CallerID network.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell your personal phone number or location data to data brokers. We rely on the following enterprise infrastructure processors:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Used for encrypted authentication (Firebase Auth), database storage (Cloud Firestore), serverless API logic (Cloud Functions), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and abuse prevention (Firebase App Check).
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK: For non-Premium users, CallerID displays App Open Ads, Interstitial Ads, and Rewarded Ads (which unlock free phone/name lookups) using Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-8674393465032299; authorized in our app-ads.txt). Google Mobile Ads may collect device identifiers (such as IDFV/IDFA where permitted by iOS settings), coarse IP-based location, and ad interaction metrics to serve and measure advertisements and prevent fraud. Upgrading to any CallerID Pro subscription immediately disables all advertisements.
  • Apple Inc. (StoreKit 2, MapKit, CallKit, APNs): Processes in-app subscription payments, map tiles, turn-by-turn routing calculations, local call directory blocking, and push notification delivery under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Legal Bases for Processing & Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Moroccan Law 09-08, our legal bases are:

  • Performance of a Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing your phone number, account profile, and StoreKit entitlements to deliver the core CallerID, CallKit blocking, and subscription features you request.
  • Explicit Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): Processing your opt-in GPS last-known location, push notifications, and optional searcher identity sharing. You may withdraw consent at any time via in-app toggles or iOS system settings.
  • Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): Protecting users from telecommunications fraud, robocalls, phishing, and abuse through crowdsourced spam scoring and security rate-limiting.

Your Rights (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Portability, Objection & Non-Discrimination): Regardless of where you reside, you have the right to request a copy of your personal data, correct inaccurate information, delete your data, object to processing, and opt out of targeted advertising. We respond to all verified privacy requests within 30 days.

8. Data Retention & Instant In-App Account Deletion

One-Tap Permanent Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v)): You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly inside the iOS app at any time by navigating to Profile > Settings > Delete Account and confirming deletion.

When you trigger in-app account deletion:

  • Our Cloud Function (deleteUserAccountAndData) immediately and irreversibly deletes your user profile document, phone registry index, saved last-known location coordinates, incoming and outgoing location requests, personal blocked number cloud records, search activity logs, and Firebase Authentication account.
  • Local guest search history and local block lists stored on your device are cleared when you clear history or uninstall the app.
  • If you no longer have the app installed, you may request complete data erasure by emailing walid.aourir1993@gmail.com with your phone number in E.164 format.

9. Phone Number Owner Unlisting & Reputation Dispute Procedure

If your phone number has been reported or labeled by community users and you believe the caller name, spam category, or reputation score is inaccurate, or if you wish to unlist your phone number from community directory lookups, you may submit a free Number Rectification / Unlisting Request via our Contact & Dispute Page or by emailing walid.aourir1993@gmail.com. Upon verifying ownership of the phone number, we will update, reset, or unlist the record within 30 days.

10. Data Security, International Transfers & Children’s Privacy

  • Security Measures: All network traffic between the CallerID iOS app and Firebase/Google Cloud is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2/1.3, and Firestore documents are protected at rest with strict server-enforced Firestore Security Rules and hashed credential storage.
  • International Transfers: Data processed via Google Firebase (United States / EU multi-region data centers) is protected under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable.
  • Children’s Privacy: CallerID is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a minor has submitted personal data, we will delete it immediately.
