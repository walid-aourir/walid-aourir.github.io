Apple StoreKit 2 Billing

Manage or Cancel a Subscription

All CallerID Pro subscriptions (Locator_weekly $3.99, Locator_monthly $5.99, Locator_yearly $69.99) are billed directly by Apple. To view, change, or cancel your plan:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions and select CallerID.
