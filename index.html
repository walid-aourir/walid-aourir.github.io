Frequently Asked Questions

Answers About CallerID, GPS Privacy & Subscriptions

Everything you need to know about how CallerID protects both your incoming calls and your legal privacy rights.

How does CallerID locate a phone number and show a path to it? ＋

CallerID operates strictly on user consent. A phone number’s location is only displayed when that registered user has explicitly enabled Public Last-Known Location Discoverability (after confirming a public disclosure prompt) or has explicitly accepted your location request inside the app. Once authorized, CallerID calculates a live driving or walking route path from your location to theirs using Apple MapKit and lets you launch turn-by-turn voice navigation in Apple Maps.

Does CallerID upload my private iPhone Contacts or record phone calls? ＋

No. CallerID never uploads or harvests your private iOS Contacts/Address Book and never intercepts or records phone call audio. Call blocking and caller identification use Apple’s sandboxed CXCallDirectoryProvider (Call Directory Extension).

How does subscription tracking and cancellation work? ＋

CallerID uses Apple’s official StoreKit 2 framework to verify active subscription entitlements (Locator_weekly, Locator_monthly, Locator_yearly) in real time and automatically removes premium status when a subscription expires or is refunded. You can manage or cancel auto-renewal at any time in iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

How can I dispute a community spam report or delete my data? ＋

If you have an account, you can permanently delete your account and all personal data immediately inside the app via Settings > Delete Account. If you are a phone number owner and wish to dispute a crowdsourced spam score, correct a caller label, or opt your number out of directory lookups, submit a request on our Contact & Dispute Center or email walid.aourir1993@gmail.com.
